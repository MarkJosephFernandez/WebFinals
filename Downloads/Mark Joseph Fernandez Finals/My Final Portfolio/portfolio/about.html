I’m pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Foundation University. My coursework gives me the foundations; building with classmates helps me put them into practice.

I’ve worked on Fuventures, a Java campus navigation guide, and FUNAV, a mobile app that brings campus maps and university resources together. With Hayahive, I’m exploring how physical certification and digital information can help students assess housing.

My contributions span interface design, databases, programming, testing, and documentation. I like understanding how the pieces connect, from what someone sees on a screen to the data behind it.
